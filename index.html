The house

A little pause
between places.

Trattino is the Italian word for the dash — the small mark that joins two things together. Our house is just that: a link between busy lives and the unhurried rhythm of the Umbrian countryside.

Built on the site of an ancient Etruscan tomb, Casa Trattino sits in its own land just outside Città della Pieve, with olive groves and fruit trees to wander through and views across the hills. Inside, it's a family home: terracotta floors, beamed ceilings, a cosy living room with a stove, and five bedrooms — all air-conditioned. Outside, there's a saltwater pool, a covered terrace for long lunches and a proper Italian grill and pizza oven for the evenings.

Location

Città della Pieve, Umbria

On the border of Umbria and Tuscany, Città della Pieve is a red-brick hill town with wonderful restaurants. Days here are for exploring nearby hill towns like Monteleone d'Orvieto, or simply staying by the pool.
